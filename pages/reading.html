Reading

Books, papers, courses, and lectures I'm working through or have found useful. One-sentence annotations where I have something to say.

Currently

  • A Mathematical Introduction to Compressive Sensing — Foucart & Rauhut Foucart, Rauhut

    Working through the linear algebra foundations. Dense but worth it.

  • In-context Learning and Induction Heads Olsson et al., Anthropic (2022)

    The clearest example I've found of mechanistic interpretability doing something concrete.

  • Toy Models of Superposition Elhage et al., Anthropic (2022)

    Why models represent more features than they have dimensions. The geometry is interesting.

Business & Operations

  • The Goal Eliyahu Goldratt

    Theory of constraints applied to manufacturing. More useful for thinking about bottlenecks than most MBA-style books.

  • High Output Management Andy Grove

    The best book on running operations I've read. Grove treats management as an engineering problem.

  • Thinking in Systems Donella Meadows

    Useful framework for understanding why businesses behave the way they do at scale.

Math & ML

  • Linear Algebra Done Right Sheldon Axler

    The right way to learn linear algebra if you care about structure over computation.

  • Deep Learning Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville

    Reference more than a read-through. Useful for the mathematical foundations.

  • Feature Visualization Olah et al., Distill (2017)

    Good starting point for understanding what early interpretability work was trying to do.
