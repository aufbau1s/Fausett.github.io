About

I'm Lewis. I've spent the last decade or so running Patrick Adair Designs, a direct-to-consumer jewelry company. Building it has given me a lot of interesting problems to work on: building a factory in China, earning a YouTube Play Button, and selling a lot of rings along the way.

I'm still running PAD while pursuing a degree in Applied Mathematics at Columbia.

The business has also opened doors outside our own company — consulting for private equity firms working on turnarounds, working with influencers, and getting to meet hundreds of operators who are building interesting things.

This site is a personal (and public) notebook to keep track of what I'm building and learning: e-commerce operations, agentic workflows, applied math, and anything else that sparks my interest.

Current questions

I'm especially interested in two things right now:

  1. How can you transform the volume and quality of output at an organization with modern agentic workflows?
  2. How can we better understand what machine learning models are actually doing?

Background

Before Patrick Adair Designs, I was a student at the University of Utah, where I worked as an undergraduate researcher at the Utah Center for Advanced Imaging Research (UCAIR). That work led to a peer-reviewed publication in medical imaging in 2020.
