About

I'm Lewis. I study Applied Mathematics at Columbia University's School of General Studies, consult on growth and operations at Reach Digital, and build direct-to-consumer brands at Tulya.

From 2017 to 2026 I was co-founder and CEO of Patrick Adair Designs, a DTC jewelry company that grew to eight figures in annual revenue. Building it meant building a factory in China, earning a YouTube Play Button, and selling a lot of rings along the way.

The business also opened doors outside our own company: consulting for private equity firms working on turnarounds, working with creators, and meeting hundreds of operators building interesting things.

This site is a personal (and public) notebook: e-commerce operations, agentic workflows, applied math, and anything else that sparks my interest. Longer term, I'm working toward a PhD in mathematics.
