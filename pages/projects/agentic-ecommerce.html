Agentic E-Commerce

What happens when you replace manual e-commerce operations with autonomous AI agents? This is the parent project for my ongoing work building agent-native systems across media buying, inventory, customer experience, and cashflow — all running inside Patrick Adair Designs as a live testbed.

// The Core Thesis

Most e-commerce "automation" is just scheduled scripts. Real agentic systems can reason about context, decide between options, and act across platforms — closing the loop between data and dollars without a human in the middle. The goal is to build those systems and document what actually works.

PAD is the proving ground. Every agent system here is running in production, handling real budget, real customers, and real inventory. The write-ups below are honest post-mortems and living documentation — not polished case studies. [Expand this section as the thesis evolves.]
