The Meta Ads Operator

// The Problem

Meta advertising for a DTC brand is a constant cycle: review performance, kill underperformers, scale winners, brief new creatives, adjust budgets. At PAD, this was consuming hours per day and was highly reactive — always catching up to what already happened.

The Meta Ads Operator is the attempt to make this proactive and autonomous — an agent that monitors the account, makes decisions, and executes them on a cadence that no human can match. [Expand with your specific context and constraints at PAD.]
