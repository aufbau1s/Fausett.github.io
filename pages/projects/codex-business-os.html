// The Premise

Codex can write code. But code is just formalized logic — and most business operations are just logic applied to data. So: what happens when you point Codex at your actual business problems and let it write the solutions?

This project is part experiment, part documentation. The goal is to find the real boundary between "Codex can handle this" and "this still needs a human" — and to build the scaffolding that makes the former as large as possible.
